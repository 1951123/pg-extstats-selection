\begin{abstract}
TODO: one-paragraph abstract. Draft from outline.md §0: we formalise the
selection of PostgreSQL extended statistics (\texttt{CREATE STATISTICS}) under a
storage budget as a sparse, budget-constrained selection problem with an
explicit \emph{capacity} dimension (each statistic's \texttt{statistics\_target}).
On single-table workloads a single dominant column combination suffices per
query, which makes the objective exactly linear and solvable with a mixed-integer
program in seconds at full-workload scale. A catalog-mask measurement protocol
makes per-candidate, per-capacity estimation feasible on wide tables. On
join-heavy workloads extended statistics cannot act, which we use as a sharp
negative control. End-to-end validation shows per-candidate prediction is sound,
and isolates the residual risk to planner cross-talk among overlapping statistics.
\end{abstract}
